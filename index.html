About Me

Hi! My name is Prajana, and welcome to my website! I'm a third-year undergraduate student at Northeastern University studying Data Science and Biochemistry. At school, I'm involved in two research labs: the SHAC Lab, where I'm working on an independent project looking at how accents and background noise affect speech intelligibility, and the Beuning Lab, where I study protein-protein interactions in DNA damage response proteins. Being involved in research has been a really cool way to explore my interests in both data science and biology. I especially enjoy seeing how the things I learn in class can be applied to real-world problems. I've also loved getting to learn new skills, work with different people, and explore topics I wouldn't normally come across in my classes. Outside of school, I love spending time with friends, weekend trips, and trying new coffee shops. I'm always looking for new places to explore, whether that's finding a cute cafe in Boston or visiting somewhere completely new. I've been to 27 states and 6 countries and hope to visit all 50 states. I made this website to share a little bit about myself, what I'm interested in, and some of the things I've been working on!

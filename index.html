About Me

Hi! My name is Prajana and welcome to my website! I am a 3rd year undergraduate student studying Data Science and Biochemistry. At school, I'm involved in 2 different research labs: the SHAC Lab where I am working on an independent project understanding how intelligibility changes with accented speech and noise and the Beuning Lab where I work on analyzing protein-protein interactions in DNA damage response proteins. Through my research experiences, I've developed a strong interest in understanding how data science can be used to better understand complex biological processes and address real world problems. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with friends, travelling, and trying new coffee shops.

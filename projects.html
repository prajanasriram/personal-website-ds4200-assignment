My Research Project

I am working on a project that looks into if intelligibility of accented speech changes in noise. There are two families of accent, L1 which are native English speakers and L2 where English is their second language. Understanding speech in background noise tends to be harder than listening to speech in clear no noise conditions. Through this study we are aiming to see if there is a difference in understanding with 2 different accents. Participants are given a hearing screening at the beginning and then the experimental task begins. They are played 120 sentences from 2 accetns are 3 noise levels creating 6 conditions. The order of the 6 conditions were randomized against participants to prevent order as a confounding variable. After the sentence is played they are expected to type out what they heard. One of the things I find really interesting about this project is getting to see how different factors can affect something we do every day, like understanding speech. I've also gotten to work on different parts of the research process, from collecting participant data to analyzing responses. It's been a great opportunity to learn more about experimental research and how data can help us better understand speech perception.
